A trusted private building engineering company in Hong Kong since 2009.

Company Introduction

Asia Pioneer Engineering Limited is a private building engineering company in Hong Kong and was founded in 2009. The company mainly engages in building construction and Alteration & Addition (A&A) works.

Apart from building projects, the company also has extensive experience in site formation and sub-structure works, underground drain works and can handle projects from site formation and foundation stage to finishing and project completion.

The company is a Registered General Building Contractor (RGBC) listed in the Buildings Department of HKSAR and has obtained ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification.

RGBC GBC 17/2015 ISO 9001:2015 ISO 14001:2015 ISO 45001:2018

Company Objective & Management Philosophy

The company's objective is to complete the projects in the most effective and efficient way to the satisfaction of all stakeholders, including the Building Clients, Consultants, Business partners, and our work teams.

The company has devoted extensive effort in work planning and monitoring in order to tackle and solve problems in advance. We treat all parties in a fair and reasonable manner to avoid unnecessary conflicts and to maintain a good and harmonic working relationship.

On the other hand, the company implements effective and strong management systems to ensure everything is executed under control.
